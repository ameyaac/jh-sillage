How It Works

This page still runs the placeholder scorer: it matches notes against a seed like/dislike list plus whatever it can infer from perfumes you have rated 4+ or 2−. It fits no parameters, so it cannot be validated — treat the number as a rough guide, not a measurement.

The real model is being built in Python and will be exported here. What matters now is that your actual ratings are recorded — they are the training data, and predictions are never learned from.

Your base smell palette

Optional. Tell the model what you already know you like, so it has somewhere to start before you have rated anything. You can skip this entirely and just start rating — an empty palette is a complete answer.

This is a starting point, not a rule. Anything you declare here is a prior: as soon as you rate real perfumes, the ratings override it. Precedence is what you rated > what you declared > the shipped default list.

Nothing here is used as training data. Declarations are saved as their own events, so changing your mind is recorded rather than erased, and setting something back to – retracts it.

Note families

The most useful thing to fill in: families transfer to notes you have never met, which is what lets the model score an unfamiliar fragrance at all.

Specific notes

Individual notes you know you seek out or can't stand. Press Enter or comma to add. A note can't be in both lists — the newer entry wins.

Medium & concentration

Formats you actually reach for. Matched exactly against the medium you pick when logging, so these stay comparable.
